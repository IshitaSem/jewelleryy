Shopping Guide 📖

1

Explore the Catalogue 🐾

Browse through Earrings, Pendants, Bracelets, Charms, Brooches, Rings, and Handmade Jewellery. Click any card to open detail view with full un-cropped photos.

2

Review Your Cart 🛒

Click "Add to Cart". You can review items, adjust quantity, or check parcel weight inside the cart sidebar anytime.

3

Provide Delivery Details 📦

Click "Proceed to Checkout" and enter your address and phone number for shipping updates.

4

Pay via UPI App 💸

Use the "Pay via UPI" button or copy our UPI ID ishsem@ptyes to complete payment.

5

Upload Payment Screenshot 📸

Take a screenshot of your successful UPI payment, upload it on checkout, and submit your order!

6

Want a custom set or personalized charm? DM us on Instagram @theglam._.aura anytime!
