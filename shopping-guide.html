How to Shop 💖

Your complete guide to buying cute, handmade chaos from The Glam Aura.

1

Explore the Catalogue 🐾

Browse through our collection of Earrings, Necklaces, Phone Charms, and more on the homepage. Click on any product to see more images and details.

2

Review Your Cart 🛒

Once you've found what you love, add it to your cart. You can open the cart sidebar at any time to review your items, change quantities, or remove things.

3

Provide Delivery Details 📦

Ready to checkout? Provide your full name, complete address, and phone number on the checkout page so we know exactly where to send your sparkle.

4

Review Invoice ✨

Double-check your order summary and total amount in the right panel of the checkout page before making a payment. Ensure all items are correct.

5

Make Your Payment 💸

Click the "Pay via UPI" button to open your preferred UPI app. The exact order total and our UPI ID are already filled in for a seamless transaction.

6

Submit Screenshot 📸

Take a screenshot of the successful payment from your UPI app. Upload the image directly on the checkout page using the file upload button.

7

Track Your Order 🚚

If you're logged in, you can view real-time status updates and courier tracking numbers in your Account dashboard. Guest buyers can simply DM us on Instagram with their Order ID.

8

Custom Requests? 🎀

Want a specific color combo or a customized charm? DM us on Instagram before placing your order, and we'll create something special just for you!

Start Shopping ✨
