Shopping Guide ✨

Your step-by-step guide to buying handcrafted jewellery from The Glam Aura.

1

Explore the Catalogue 🐾

Browse through our collection of Earrings, Necklaces, Bracelets, Phone Charms, and Brooches. Click on any product card to open the detail view with full un-cropped images.

2

Review Your Cart 🛒

Add your favorite items to your cart. Click the floating or header cart button at any time to open your cart sidebar, adjust item quantities, or check shipping weight.

3

Provide Delivery Details 📦

Click "Proceed to Checkout" and enter your full name, complete address, pincode, and phone number so we can dispatch your parcel safely.

4

Review Invoice ✨

Verify your total amount, parcel weight, and shipping charges in the Order Summary panel before proceeding to payment.

5

Make Your Payment 💸

Click "Pay via UPI Mobile App" or copy our UPI ID ishsem@ptyes to complete prepayment using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM.

6

Submit Screenshot 📸

Take a screenshot of your successful UPI transaction. Attach the image file on the checkout page and click "Place Order".

7

Order Confirmation & Tracking 🚚

Save your unique Order Number (JEW-XXXXX). We process handmade orders in 10-15 days and dispatch via India Post with tracking updates.

8

Custom Requests & Help 🎀

Looking for a custom color combination or special request? Direct Message us on Instagram anytime!
